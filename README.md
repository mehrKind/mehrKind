## 🧠 About Me

- 🎓 M.Sc. student in **Artificial Intelligence** at Jahrom University, focused on **computer vision** and **multimodal AI**
- 🔬 Currently developing **MehrVQA**, a question-adaptive, neuro-symbolic Visual Question Answering pipeline — my master's thesis, combining targeted visual grounding with LLM-guided reasoning
- 📄 Author of an IEEE-style paper on a **neuro-symbolic facial emotion recognition** pipeline (fuzzy logic + ViT embeddings + XGBoost on RAF-DB, 82%+ accuracy)
- 💻 4+ years building full-stack products — from Persian RTL enterprise dashboards to 3D interactive web experiences
- 🎯 Long-term goal: pursue a **PhD** and grow into an AI researcher-engineer who ships real products, publishes research, and eventually builds a technology company
- 🌐 Comfortable working end-to-end: research → prototype → production, in both English and Persian (RTL) contexts
